## Analysis and stop rule

Primary Recall@3 by case, plus original ranking metrics. Paired comparisons: each trained rule minus its untrained counterpart, and trained absolute minus squared-input control. Deterministic2000-resample percentile95% seed-bootstrap intervals, exploratory/unadjusted. Report trained-vs-input absolute score Spearman agreement as descriptive evidence of similarity, not causal proof. Save per-feature signed mean-error changes to examine decreases directly. Training loss reduction establishes optimization occurred, not useful localization.

Do not pool conditions into a winning headline. For an affirmative 'learning helps consistently' conclusion require positive paired Recall@3 intervals against both untrained-absolute and squared-input controls in at least two shift families on both synthetic and real-data conditions, without concealing regressions; this is a conservative decision heuristic, not a preregistered confirmatory test or novelty criterion. If unmet, stop pursuing a generally superior reconstruction-attribution method here and retain the engineering project plus reproducible limitation evidence. A negative-results paper still requires a distinct contribution and prior-art assessment; this experiment alone does not justify arXiv submission.

Test-first implementation, full regression/lint checks and independent reviews precede a clean committed real run. Verify resume before saving the final decision report. End this investigation with a decision; no automatic additional experiment or method expansion.
